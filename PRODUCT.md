# Log Horizon

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product purpose
A local desktop diary for personal writing. Freewrite is a continuous page; Pile is a chronological stream of momentary thoughts, with a stationary writing area and editable thoughts scrolling above it. A pile may span days.

## Users and constraints
Single local user. No accounts, network services, AI, streaks, or collaboration. Existing diary entries must survive migration. Selected entries and entire piles can be private under one diary password. Dates and the existence of private entries remain visible.

## Current direction
A writing-first space with warm paper and cool graphite surfaces, rounded compact controls, system sans typography, a centered borderless page, and an animated entry drawer. The header contains only navigation and an actions menu; creation, settings, and privacy actions stay inside menus. Pile uses small labelled icons for editing and deletion. The supplied design reference informs control sizing and radii. Freewrite remains inspiration for low friction. No decorative prompts, date headers, chat bubbles, forced titles, or textarea focus box. Saving uses a small check or delayed spinner; word count is optional.

## Security boundary
Authenticated encryption at rest for private titles and content. Conceal on losing focus; lock private sessions automatically. Ordinary entries remain plaintext. Historical exports and backups cannot be retroactively protected. No forgotten-password recovery in this version.
